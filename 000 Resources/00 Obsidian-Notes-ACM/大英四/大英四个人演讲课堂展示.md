# 大英四个人演讲课堂展示

## Unit 1 The true value of education

Good morning, everyone. Today I’d like to talk about the true value of education.

Some people think college education is mainly about getting a good job. I agree that a good job is important, but it is not the only purpose. As a freshman, I study hard and take part in algorithm competitions. Through programming, I have learned patience, logical thinking, and problem-solving skills. These abilities are useful not only for my future career, but also for life.

In my opinion, education teaches us how to think, how to learn, and how to face challenges. A good job may be one result, but personal growth is the real value.

Thank you for listening.

---

## Unit 2 The myth of a dream job

Good morning, everyone. Today I’d like to talk about my dream job.

My dream job is to become a software engineer, especially in algorithms or artificial intelligence. As a freshman, I study hard in my courses and take part in algorithm competitions in my spare time. These experiences make me more interested in programming.

I choose this job because I enjoy coding and problem-solving. Programming is logical, creative, and useful. It can help people solve real problems and make life easier. Also, I like challenges. When I solve a difficult algorithm problem, I feel a strong sense of achievement.

Although this job may be stressful, I believe my interest and hard work can help me succeed.

Thank you for listening.

---

## Unit 4 Explore the unknown

Good morning, everyone. Today I’d like to share my experience of exploring the unknown.

For me, exploration does not only mean going to space or discovering new places. It can also happen when I solve programming problems. As a freshman, I study hard, take part in algorithm competitions, and learn programming deeply.

In competitions, I often meet problems that I cannot solve at first. I need to read carefully, think logically, try different methods, and correct my mistakes. Sometimes it is difficult, but when I finally solve the problem, I feel excited.

I think curiosity, patience, and courage make me a good explorer. Programming is my way to explore the unknown.

Thank you for listening.

---

## Unit 6 Live longer, live better

Good morning, everyone. Today I’d like to talk about how lifestyle choices affect longevity and quality of life.

In my opinion, living longer is not only about age, but also about health and happiness. As a freshman, I study hard and take part in algorithm competitions. I often spend much time sitting in front of the computer, writing code or solving problems. So I know health is very important.

If we stay up late, sit too long, or eat badly, we may feel tired and less focused. Therefore, we should get enough sleep, exercise regularly, eat a balanced diet, and learn to relax.

Healthy habits can help us live longer and live better.

Thank you for listening.

---

## Unit 8 The beauty of literature

Good morning, everyone. Today I’d like to talk about the purposes of literature reading for college students.

Some students may think literature is not very useful, especially for students interested in science or technology. As a freshman who spends much time on programming and algorithm competitions, I once had a similar idea. But now I think literature is also important.

First, literature helps us understand different people, cultures, and feelings. Second, it improves our language ability and expression. Third, it helps us relax after study or coding.

In my opinion, literature makes us more thoughtful, creative, and open-minded. It is not just for exams, but for personal growth.

Thank you for listening.

## 通用话题 2：Exploration in Programming

**适用：Unit 4，也可用于 Unit 1、Unit 2**

Good morning, everyone. Today I’d like to talk about exploration in programming.

For me, programming is a kind of exploration. As a freshman, I study hard and take part in algorithm competitions. When I solve algorithm problems, I often do not know the answer at first. I need to read carefully, try different methods, make mistakes, and improve my solution step by step.

This process is sometimes difficult, but also interesting. I think curiosity, patience, and persistence are important for an explorer. Every time I solve a problem, I am exploring something new.

Thank you for listening.

## 通用话题 3：Balanced Development in College

**适用：Unit 6、Unit 8，也可用于 Unit 1**

Good morning, everyone. Today I’d like to talk about balanced development in college.

As a freshman, I study hard, take part in algorithm competitions, and spend much time learning programming. These things are important, but college life should not only be about study and skills.

Health is also necessary. If I sit too long or stay up late, I may feel tired. Literature reading is helpful too. It helps us understand others and relax after study.

In my opinion, college students should develop both professional skills and personal qualities. That is the key to a better life.

Thank you for listening.

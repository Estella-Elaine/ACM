# 大英四双人讨论课堂展示
## 一、现场随机搭档万能控场句

### 1. 开始讨论

**A:** Hi, let’s discuss this topic together. Maybe we can make a simple but practical plan. 

你好，我们一起讨论一下这个话题吧。我们可以制定一个简单但实用的计划。

**B:** Sure. I think we should first decide the main idea, then discuss the details. 

当然。我觉得我们可以先确定主要想法，再讨论细节。

---

### 2. 询问对方意见

**A:** What do you think about it? 

你怎么看？

**B:** That sounds good. I’d like to add one more point. 

听起来不错。我想补充一点。

**A:** Do you have any suggestions about the time or place? 

关于时间或地点你有什么建议吗？

---

### 3. 对方说得少时，你可以继续引导

**A:** Good point. Maybe we can make it more specific. 

说得好。也许我们可以说得更具体一点。

**A:** For example, we can choose Saturday morning because most students are free. 

比如，我们可以选择周六上午，因为大多数学生有空。

**A:** Let’s also think about the purpose of this activity. 

我们也想一下这个活动的目的。

---

### 4. 表示同意

**B:** I agree with you. 

我同意你的看法。

**B:** That’s a practical idea. 

这是个很实际的想法。

**B:** Yes, and it will be convenient for students. 

是的，而且对学生来说很方便。

---

### 5. 结尾总结

**A:** So, to sum up, our plan includes the time, place, activities, and purpose. 

总之，我们的计划包括时间、地点、活动和目的。

**B:** I think this plan is clear and meaningful. 

我认为这个计划很清楚，也很有意义。

---

## 二、十个话题90秒双人对话稿

---

### Topic 1 Travel Plan 

#### Three-day sightseeing plan in Hangzhou

**A:** Hi, we have three days to go sightseeing in Hangzhou. Let’s make a travel plan.

**B:** Good idea. Hangzhou is famous for its natural beauty and culture. Where should we go first?

**A:** On the first day, I think we can visit the West Lake. It is the most famous place in Hangzhou.

**B:** I agree. We can walk around the lake, visit Broken Bridge and Su Causeway, and take some photos.

**A:** In the evening, we can watch the West Lake music fountain if possible.

**B:** Sounds great. What about the second day?

**A:** We can go to Lingyin Temple in the morning and Longjing Tea Village in the afternoon.

**B:** That’s nice. We can enjoy the quiet environment and learn about Chinese tea culture.

**A:** For the third day, I suggest visiting Xixi Wetland. It is a good place to relax and enjoy nature.

**B:** I like that. What transportation should we use?

**A:** We can mainly take the subway and buses. For short distances, we can ride shared bikes.

**B:** That will be convenient and cheap.

**A:** So our plan includes the West Lake, Lingyin Temple, Longjing Tea Village, and Xixi Wetland.

**B:** Yes. It will be a relaxing and meaningful trip.

---

### Topic 2 Volunteer Activity 

#### Organizing a volunteer activity

**A:** Hi, let’s talk about organizing a volunteer activity. Do you have any ideas?

**B:** Maybe we can organize a community cleaning activity near our university.

**A:** Good idea. When and where should we hold it?

**B:** I think this Saturday morning is suitable. The place can be the park near our campus.

**A:** I agree. Many students are free on Saturday, and the park is easy to reach.

**B:** How can we recruit students?

**A:** We can post notices in class WeChat groups and put up posters near the canteen and dormitories.

**B:** We can also ask the Students’ Union to help us.

**A:** Yes. During the activity, volunteers can pick up rubbish, clean public seats, and put up environmental signs.

**B:** We should prepare gloves, rubbish bags, and drinking water.

**A:** Good point. What is the purpose of this activity?

**B:** It can improve the community environment and raise students’ sense of responsibility.

**A:** I agree. It can also help students develop teamwork skills.

**B:** So this activity is useful for both students and the community.

**A:** Exactly. I think it will be meaningful.

---

### Topic 3 Lecture on Healthy Living 

#### Hosting a lecture on healthy living

**A:** Hi, let’s discuss how to host a lecture on healthy living.

**B:** Sure. First, who should we invite?

**A:** I think we can invite a doctor from the university hospital.

**B:** Good choice. A doctor can give professional advice on sleep, diet, exercise, and mental health.

**A:** Yes. Many college students stay up late or sit for a long time. For example, I often spend time programming and preparing for algorithm competitions, so I need to pay attention to health.

**B:** That’s true. When and where should we hold the lecture?

**A:** How about next Wednesday evening at 7 p.m. in the lecture hall?

**B:** That sounds suitable. Most students are free in the evening.

**A:** How can we publicize it on campus?

**B:** We can make posters and put them near the canteen, library, and dormitories.

**A:** We can also share the information in WeChat groups and add a QR code for registration.

**B:** Good idea. The lecture can help students build healthier habits.

**A:** Yes. It can remind us that health is the foundation of study and life.

**B:** So our lecture will be practical and meaningful.

---

### Topic 4 Conducting a Survey 

#### Survey on students’ lifestyle

**A:** Hi, let’s talk about conducting a survey. What topic should we choose?

**B:** I think we can do a survey on college students’ lifestyle.

**A:** Good idea. It is closely related to our daily life. Who will be the participants?

**B:** We can invite freshmen and sophomores from different majors.

**A:** That will make the results more balanced. What questions should we ask?

**B:** We can ask: How many hours do you sleep every day? How often do you exercise? Do you eat breakfast regularly?

**A:** We can also ask how much time they spend on phones or computers every day.

**B:** Good point. Many students spend too much time on screens.

**A:** Yes, especially students like me who often program or prepare for algorithm competitions.

**B:** When and where should we conduct the survey?

**A:** We can do it online this weekend. We can also interview students near the library.

**B:** Online surveys are convenient, and face-to-face interviews may give us more details.

**A:** After collecting the data, we can make charts and analyze the results.

**B:** The purpose is to understand students’ habits and give suggestions for healthier living.

**A:** Exactly. This survey will be practical.

---

### Topic 5 Building a Fitness Center 

#### Building a campus fitness center

**A:** Hi, let’s discuss building a fitness center on campus.

**B:** Great. First, where should it be?

**A:** I think it should be near the dormitory area, because students can go there easily.

**B:** I agree. What about the size?

**A:** Maybe about 500 square meters is enough at the beginning.

**B:** That sounds practical. What facilities should it have?

**A:** It should have treadmills, exercise bikes, strength training machines, yoga mats, and table tennis tables.

**B:** We can also have a small area for stretching and relaxing.

**A:** Good idea. Many students sit for a long time every day. I often study programming, so I really need more exercise.

**B:** Yes. A fitness center can help students build healthy habits.

**A:** How can we raise money for it?

**B:** We can ask the university for support and look for sponsorship from local companies.

**A:** Maybe students can also pay a small membership fee, but it should be affordable.

**B:** I agree. The main purpose is to improve students’ health, not to make money.

**A:** So this plan is useful and realistic.

### Topic 6 Museum Tour 

#### Organizing a tour to a museum

**A:** Hi, let’s talk about organizing a tour to a museum.

**B:** Good idea. When and where should we go?

**A:** We can visit the Zhejiang Provincial Museum this Saturday morning.

**B:** That sounds nice. It has many historical and cultural exhibits.

**A:** We can meet at the school gate at 8:30 a.m. and take the subway together.

**B:** What should we do during the tour?

**A:** First, we can listen to a guide’s introduction. Then we can visit different exhibition halls in groups.

**B:** We can also take notes and photos of important exhibits.

**A:** After the visit, each group can share something they learned.

**B:** That will make the activity more meaningful.

**A:** How can this tour boost our cultural confidence?

**B:** By learning about Chinese history, art, and traditional culture, we can better understand our country.

**A:** I agree. Museums are not just places for old objects. They tell stories about our civilization.

**B:** Exactly. This tour can help us learn outside the classroom.

**A:** So it is both educational and interesting.

### Topic 7 Finding an Internship 

#### Discussion about finding an internship

**A:** Hi, let’s discuss how to find an internship.

**B:** Sure. I think students can search on recruitment websites and company official accounts.

**A:** Yes. We can also ask teachers, seniors, or career advisors for information.

**B:** What kind of internship are you interested in?

**A:** Since I’m interested in programming and algorithm competitions, I would like to find an internship related to software development.

**B:** That fits your background. You can prepare a resume and include your programming projects and competition experience.

**A:** Good advice. What can we learn from an internship?

**B:** We can learn practical skills, workplace communication, teamwork, and how companies solve real problems.

**A:** I agree. At school, we mainly learn knowledge from books, but an internship connects theory with practice.

**B:** What is the purpose of having an internship?

**A:** It helps us understand the workplace and make better career plans.

**B:** It can also help us know whether a job is suitable for us.

**A:** Exactly. So an internship is not only for a resume, but also for personal growth.

**B:** Well said.

### Topic 8 Book Drive 

#### Running a book donation activity

**A:** Hi, let’s talk about running a book drive.

**B:** Good idea. Who can donate books?

**A:** All students and teachers can donate books. Many people have books they no longer use.

**B:** Where should they donate the books?

**A:** We can set up donation boxes in the library, teaching buildings, and dormitory areas.

**B:** What kinds of books should we collect?

**A:** Textbooks, storybooks, English books, science books, and children’s books are all welcome.

**B:** Where will the books go?

**A:** We can donate them to rural primary schools or community reading rooms.

**B:** That’s meaningful. Books can help children learn more about the world.

**A:** How can we promote the event?

**B:** We can make posters, short videos, and share the information in class groups.

**A:** We can also ask volunteers to introduce the event in different classes.

**B:** What is the purpose of the book drive?

**A:** It encourages reading, reduces waste, and shares knowledge with people in need.

**B:** I agree. A used book can still bring new value to others.

### Topic 9 Speech on Chinese Culture 

#### Making a speech to introduce Chinese culture

**A:** Hi, let’s discuss making a speech to introduce Chinese culture.

**B:** Good idea. What topic should we choose?

**A:** I suggest Chinese tea culture. It is traditional, elegant, and easy to understand.

**B:** I like that. Tea is common in daily life, but it also has deep cultural meaning.

**A:** Who will be the audience?

**B:** We can deliver the speech to international students at our university.

**A:** Then we should use simple English and add pictures or short videos.

**B:** What should the speech include?

**A:** We can introduce the history of tea, different types of Chinese tea, and basic tea-making steps.

**B:** We can also talk about the spirit of harmony and respect in tea culture.

**A:** Good point. Maybe we can prepare a short tea-tasting activity.

**B:** That will make the speech more interactive.

**A:** What is the purpose of this speech?

**B:** It can help international students understand Chinese culture better.

**A:** It can also improve our cultural confidence and English expression.

**B:** Exactly. We are learning how to tell Chinese stories to the world.

### Topic 10 Career Planning 

#### Making career planning

**A:** Hi, let’s talk about career planning. Why is it important?

**B:** Career planning helps students set clear goals and prepare for the future.

**A:** I agree. Without a plan, we may feel confused or waste time.

**B:** How can we make career planning?

**A:** First, we should understand ourselves, including our interests, strengths, and weaknesses.

**B:** For example, you are interested in programming and algorithm competitions, so software engineering may be a suitable direction.

**A:** Yes. Second, we should learn about different jobs and industries.

**B:** Third, we need to improve our skills step by step.

**A:** These skills include professional knowledge, communication, teamwork, and problem-solving ability.

**B:** What help can students seek when making career planning?

**A:** We can ask teachers, seniors, career advisors, and people who already work in the industry.

**B:** We can also attend career lectures, internship fairs, and company talks.

**A:** Good point. Internships are helpful too, because they allow us to experience real work.

**B:** So career planning is not just about choosing a job.

**A:** Right. It is about understanding ourselves, setting goals, and taking action.

## 四、你现场可以采用的“万能结构”

如果你临场忘词，就按这个顺序说：

### 任何计划类话题都可以这样答：

**Step 1: Decide the main idea** 

First, we should decide what activity we want to organize.

**Step 2: Decide time and place** 

I think Saturday morning / Wednesday evening is suitable. 

The place can be the library / lecture hall / community park / museum.

**Step 3: Decide participants** 

The participants can be our classmates, students from different majors, or international students.

**Step 4: Decide activities** 

During the activity, we can listen, discuss, take notes, share ideas, or do volunteer work.

**Step 5: Publicize or recruit** 

We can post notices in WeChat groups, put up posters, and ask the Students’ Union for help.

**Step 6: Explain purpose** 

The purpose is to help students learn more, improve their abilities, and make campus life more meaningful.

## 五、最推荐你重点背的三句话

这三句很多话题都能用：

1. **I think this plan is practical because it is easy to organize and students can take part in it conveniently.** 

我认为这个计划很实际，因为它容易组织，学生参加也方便。

2. **The purpose of this activity is not only to solve a problem, but also to improve students’ sense of responsibility.** 

这个活动的目的不仅是解决问题，也能提升学生的责任感。

3. **As a freshman who is interested in programming and algorithm competitions, I believe planning, teamwork, and problem-solving skills are very important.** 

作为一名对编程和算法竞赛感兴趣的大一学生，我认为规划、合作和解决问题的能力非常重要。
